## Full description (both stores)
The National Standards for the Physical Inspection of Real Estate (NSPIRE)
Self-Inspection App is a method for conducting and processing inspections and
assessments of multi-unit apartment and housing developments — before purchase,
or while managing and improving confidence in an owner/management's ability to
keep a property in compliance.

NSPIRE INSPECTION (Public) helps you:
- Accurately assess a unit's condition against NSPIRE standards
- Implement streamlined, structured inspection processes
- Prioritize the health and safety of residents
- Record deficiencies by category (Outside, Inside, and Unit areas) with photos
- Track inspection progress across buildings and units in real time
- Generate and export inspection reports

Built for property managers, inspectors, and housing authorities working with
public and affordable housing under NSPIRE (National Standards for the
Physical Inspection of Real Estate).
